About Us

InAmigos Foundation was founded on 23 September 2020 by Mr. Govind Shukla (Founder & CEO). It is a Section 8 registered non-profit organisation based in Chhattisgarh, India, working through volunteers, professionals and partners to bring positive change to society.

Our Mission

To direct every contribution towards essential causes such as food distribution, education, women empowerment, animal welfare, environmental sustainability and social inclusion across India.

Our Vision

A more inclusive, compassionate and empowered society, built through collective action.

Our commitment spans education, women empowerment, animal welfare, environmental protection and community development.

Our Ongoing Projects

Project Bachpanshala

Education and child development

Quality learning support for underprivileged children.

Objectives: Improve access to education and support child development.

Social purpose: Give every child a fair start in life.

Project Udaan

Women empowerment and girls' education

Skill development and financial independence for women and girls.

Objectives: Promote girls' education and build practical skills.

Social purpose: Help women lead independent, confident lives.

Project Jeev

Animal welfare and protection

Rescue, protection and feeding of animals in need.

Objectives: Care for stray and injured animals and encourage kindness.

Social purpose: Build a compassionate relationship with all living beings.

Project Seva

Community support and donation initiatives

Food and clothing support for underprivileged communities.

Objectives: Organise donation drives and basic-needs support.

Social purpose: Serve humanity with compassion.

Project Prakriti

Environmental conservation

Plantation and sustainability efforts for a greener future.

Objectives: Plant trees and raise environmental awareness.

Social purpose: Protect nature for future generations.

Project Vikas

Youth empowerment and skill development

Skill programmes that improve employability for young people.

Objectives: Provide training and guidance for career readiness.

Social purpose: Prepare youth to contribute to society and the economy.
